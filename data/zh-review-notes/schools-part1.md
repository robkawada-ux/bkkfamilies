## Ambiguous English and how I resolved it
- "Founded in 2003 by Julie Sutanto with just four students on a single campus" : kept as one campus at founding, three campuses now.
- Bangkok Patana "close to 40 acres" : 接近40英亩, no conversion.
- Berkeley "a decade and a half of operating history now" : 十五年, as the English says.
- Brighton Vibhavadi "organised into a Prep School for ages 3 to 11" : ages kept exactly.
- Ascot "Football academy ... higher level coaching" : 水平更高的训练.
- Bangkok Prep "highest level of that international environmental education award" : kept as 最高级别.
- The English repeats phrases like "genuinely" and "specifically" often; I rendered them with 真正 and 特别 where natural and dropped some repetitions without changing meaning.
