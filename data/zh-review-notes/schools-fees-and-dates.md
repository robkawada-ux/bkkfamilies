# Review notes: school fees and term dates (/zh)

Files: src/lib/zh/schoolFees.ts, src/lib/zh/schoolBreaks.ts. All figures and dates come from the English data; only words are translated.

## Terms to double-check
- Nursery is written 托儿班 (Nursery) and Kindergarten 幼儿园 in every label and note; Elementary and Primary 小学, Middle 初中, High 高中. "Primary 1-3" at Singapore International School is "小学 Primary 1至3" so the school's own token survives.
- Pre-Nursery, Pre-Kindergarten, Pre-K, Pre-Reception, Reception, Foundation, EY, K1 to K3, PK and Mini Dragons are left in English on purpose (school wording that parents search for).
- Half day and full day: 半日制 and 全日制. Toddler: 幼儿班 (Toddler). Lion Cubs parent-toddler: "Lion Cubs 家长陪同幼儿班".
- Extras: 周寄宿 (Boarding, weekly) and 全寄宿 (Boarding, full) are my reading of weekly and full boarding. 资本评估费 (Capital assessment fee) and 年度资本费 (Annual capital fee) are literal. 学位保证金 (Place guarantee deposit), 重新入学保证金 (Re-enrolment security), 关爱支持 (Nurture support).
- Acronyms kept as written: EAL, ESL, ELL, ELS, EFL, EIP, ECAs, IGCSE, IB, A-Level. EAL gets "(英语作为额外语言)" in each note that mentions it; in short extra labels it is just "EAL 支持".
- 候补池费用 for "Waiting pool fee"; 候补费 in the King's note for "waitlist fee".
- 强制 for "compulsory" and "mandatory"; 住宿式外出活动 for "residential trips".
- 宋干节 (Songkran), 万佛节 (Makha Bucha), 拉玛九世国王纪念日, 受难日 (Good Friday): established forms, English kept in brackets.
- Window label "Winter and New Year 2026/27" is "2026/27冬季及新年假期".

## Kept in English on purpose
School and campus names (On Nut, Thong Lo, Bang Na, Chonburi, Wang Thonglang, Sukhumvit, Green Valley, Pracha Uthit, Krungthep Kreetha, Vibhavadi, Dusit, Sathorn), programme names (Life Centered Education, Before One, Sixth Form, Explore the Kingdom, International Bilingual, International), the admissions email address, PDF.js, version numbers.

## Ambiguous or odd English and how it was resolved
- Shrewsbury: the English says cash at entry is 455,000 but 225,000 plus 225,000 is 450,000 (the extra 5,000 is presumably the application fee). Translated as written; the English may deserve a look.
- St Andrews: "Can be paid as 35,000 per term instead." does not say what can be paid that way (presumably the enrolment fee). Kept equally open: "也可改为每学期缴纳35,000".
- Garden and Wells notes use "Enrolment" for the registration fee; translated as 入学费.
- Notes carry bare figures with no currency in English; they have no 泰铢 added in Chinese, except where the English says THB (ISB: "约2,000,000泰铢").
- XCL note calls ELL "compulsory" while the fee list marks it optional; translated as written.
- Ranges such as "80-85%" and "3-6%" became "80至85%" and "3至6%". "1-2 or 3 years ahead" (RBIS) became "提前1至2年或3年".
- Berkeley "photo outreach": rendered as the email we sent asking schools for photos.
- Amnuay Silpa row labels keep the English programme name and add Chinese: "(Bilingual 双语)", "(International 国际)".

## Checker notes
Digit check reports four expected differences: "Term 2" and "Term 3" are written 第二学期 and 第三学期 in Chinese, so the numeral 2 or 3 is a word, not a missing figure.
