## Place names
- Kept as English: Bang Kapi, Prawet, Pathum Thani, Thanyaburi, Dusit, Wang Thonglang, Silom, Sathorn, Sukhumvit (including "Sukhumvit Soi 39" and "Sukhumvit 107", which are address-style names).

## Terms to double-check
- 基督复临安息日会 for Seventh-day Adventist (RAIS). Check this is the usual mainland rendering.
- "rai" written as 莱（rai）, first mention in each write-up (Thai International School, Siam International School).
- 实验学校 for "demonstration school" (Satit Pattana). The English "Satit" is kept.
- 中文（普通话） for "Mandarin Chinese" in British Mandarin International School; the language field says 中文 to match the English "Chinese".
- 寄宿 / 走读 for boarding / day places (Wycombe Abbey).
- 经 WASC 认证 for "WASC-accredited"; 剑桥国际考试（Cambridge International Examinations）考点 for the exam center.
- "K-12" kept as written. "Nursery" in age ranges written 托儿班（Nursery）; "Nursery to Year 13" field uses the brief's form 托儿班至 Year 13.

## Ambiguous English and how I resolved it
- Kids Academy: "similar to Tiny Tots, KiddyKare, or Annabel's" kept as a list of names, no Chinese forms.
- Aster: the first sentence in English is a fragment ("A newer addition ..."). Translated as a sentence starting 这是曼谷学校界的新成员.
- Satit Pattana: "Satit, or demonstration school" rendered as the Satit network, 实验学校, as the English says.
- Wycombe Abbey: "roots going back to 1896" kept as 历史可追溯到1896年.
